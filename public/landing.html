    Questions finance teams ask

    Is Wayline a bank?

    No. Wayline is a software platform. Banking-adjacent services such as fiat conversion and money transmission are provided by our regulated partners.

    What is OUSD?

    OUSD is a US dollar stablecoin issued by Bridge and operated by Open Standard. It isn’t a bank deposit and isn’t FDIC-insured. Read the issuer’s disclosures for reserve information.
